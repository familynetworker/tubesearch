Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: November 9, 2026

TermsPrivacyRefunds & cancellationEarnings disclaimerAffiliate disclosure

We believe you should always know when someone earns money from a recommendation. Here’s how that works around Tube Search.

Our partners (ambassadors)

Tube Search has a partner program. Partners, including members, creators and our own team, can share Tube Search with a personal link. If you buy through a partner’s link, that partner may earn a commission from us. It doesn’t change the price you pay.

Partners are required to tell their audience when they earn a commission, to be honest about Tube Search and to never promise results or income. If you see a partner breaking these rules, please tell us at support@squeezify.com.

Links and tools we mention

We sometimes mention or link to other products and services, such as editing tools, AI tools or equipment. Some of those links may be affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. We only recommend things we think are genuinely useful, and we’ll label affiliate links where we use them.

Sponsors shown in the app

Brands that appear in the Sponsor Finder are companies that have sponsored YouTube channels, found in public video descriptions. They are not our sponsors, they haven’t paid to appear, and we’re not connected to them.
