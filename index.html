On camera

Like your face? Show it.

Tube Search started as a faceless tool. Then the on-camera folks started knocking.

Here’s what happened. People who show their face kept telling us, “Love it. But I’m not hiding behind stock footage. I want to be on the video.” Fair enough. So we built them their own side of Tube Search.

Now you can find growing channels where a real human talks to the camera, in your exact niche. Car dealers. Fitness coaches. Cooks. Commentators. Paste your own channel and we’ll show you the growing channels most like yours, so you can see exactly what the folks a few steps ahead of you are doing differently.

Then we help with the scary part. You know, actually being on camera. Questions

Straight answers

I’ve never made a YouTube video. Is this for me?

Yes. Honestly, beginners usually get the most out of it, because you get to skip the part where you make 30 videos in the wrong niche. Ouch. The quiz picks your niche, the 9-step plan walks you through it, and the Community and live calls are there when you get stuck.

I already have a channel. Will it still help?

That’s where the Pro tools shine. X-Ray your competitors, run Channel Doctor on your own channel, follow a weekly growth plan, and use Sponsor Finder to see who’s paying channels your size.

Is it only for faceless channels?

Nope. There’s a whole side for on-camera creators: channels to learn from, Channels like mine, a script coach, a teleprompter and a face thumbnail lab.

Will this make me money?

We can’t promise that. And anybody who does is fibbing. Tube Search helps you pick better topics and make better videos. Your results depend on you, your effort and plenty of things nobody controls. Any earnings shown are estimates. Read our earnings disclaimer.

Isn’t copying other channels a bad idea?

Copying videos is a terrible idea, and we say so constantly. (Like, annoyingly often.) We show you the format: the kind of topic, the title style, the length, the posting pace. Your videos are your own.

What’s in the training and community?

Every plan gets the Creator Community (feed, chat, wins, Q&A), live webinars and a team in the chat. Premiere adds the faceless YouTube channel course, Premiere-only lessons and two live coaching calls a month.

What computer do I need?

There’s a Windows app, and a web version that works in any modern browser on Mac, iPad and Chromebook.

Where does the data come from?

Public YouTube channel and video information, gathered and checked every week. Earnings and some other numbers are estimates and labeled that way. Tube Search isn’t made by, or connected to, YouTube or Google.

Can I cancel, or get a refund?

Cancel any time from your account. And if it’s not for you, email us within 30 days of your first payment for a refund of that payment. Details in our refund policy.
