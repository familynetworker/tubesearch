Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

TermsPrivacyRefunds & cancellationEarnings disclaimerAffiliate disclosure

This policy explains what information Tube Search collects, how we use it and the choices you have. In these pages, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Tube Search, made by AI Creators & Builders. “You” means the person using Tube Search. We don’t sell your personal information.

1. What we collect

Things you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored scrambled, never in plain text).
  • Payment details: handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email, plan, billing country and payment status, but never your full card number.
  • What you share: Community posts, comments, photos, chat and support messages, questions you send to webinars, your profile and settings.
  • Sign-ups: if you register for a free webinar or training, your name and email, plus anything you choose to tell us.

Things collected when you use Tube Search

  • Device and sign-in information: a device ID for each computer or browser, device type and operating system, the computer’s network hardware ID (to recognise the same computer), app version, IP address, internet provider and approximate location worked out from the IP address (city or region level). We use this to run your license, enforce device limits, detect account sharing and keep accounts secure.
  • Usage information: which features you use and when, for example to show your progress and badges, improve the app and understand which features help.
  • Live webinars: if you turn on your camera or microphone in a live room, your audio and video are sent through our video provider to other people in the room. Hosts may record sessions; you’ll be told when that happens.
  • Alerts: if you turn on notifications, your browser gives us a push address so we can send them. You can turn them off any time.

Cookies and local storage

We use a few essential cookies to keep you signed in to the members area and to remember who referred you if you came through a partner’s link. The app and website store your settings (like your theme and progress) on your own device. We don’t use advertising cookies or sell tracking data. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers.

2. How we use it

  • To provide Tube Search: your account, license, devices, features, Community, webinars and support.
  • To take payments, prevent fraud and enforce our Terms, including stopping account sharing.
  • To send you service messages (receipts, security notices, important changes) and, if you allow it, alerts and updates. You can opt out of marketing messages any time.
  • To credit partners (ambassadors) who referred you, and pay their commissions.
  • To understand and improve Tube Search.
  • To meet legal duties.

3. Who we share it with

We share information only with services that help us run Tube Search, and only what they need:

  • Stripe for payments and subscriptions.
  • Cloudflare for hosting, our database, file storage and live video.
  • GitHub to deliver app downloads and the web version.
  • Google for YouTube API Services and website fonts.
  • Email providers to send account emails, when enabled.
  • Partners (ambassadors): if you sign up through a partner’s link, that partner may see that you signed up and, for free trainings, your name and email so they can follow up.
  • Claude: only if you choose to connect Tube Search to your own Claude account. Your questions and the answers then go through your Claude account under Anthropic’s terms.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it, or to protect people’s safety or our rights.

Other members can see what you post in the Community and your public profile.

4. YouTube API Services

Tube Search uses YouTube API Services to look up public channel and video information. Google’s use of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy, and use of YouTube features is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service. Tube Search does not ask for access to your own YouTube or Google account. If we add that in the future, we’ll update this policy first and explain how to revoke access through your Google security settings.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is active and for as long as needed afterwards for legal, tax and accounting reasons. Sign-in and security logs are kept for a limited time. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, except what we must keep by law. Community posts may stay but are no longer linked to your profile.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See, update or download the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Turn off alerts and marketing emails.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have extra rights, such as objecting to certain uses or limiting how your information is used. We honour these rights and won’t treat you differently for using them.

Email contact@squeezify.com to make a request. We may need to confirm it’s you first.

7. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, scrambled passwords and limited access. No system is perfect, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

Tube Search is not meant for children under 13, and plans are sold only to adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. International users

We’re based in the United States and our service providers may process information in other countries. By using Tube Search you understand your information may be processed outside where you live, with the protections described here.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are important, we’ll tell you by email or in the app before they take effect.
