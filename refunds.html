Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

TermsPrivacyRefunds & cancellationEarnings disclaimerAffiliate disclosure
The short version: cancel any time from your account in a couple of clicks. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for, and you won’t be charged again.

Cancelling

  • Sign in to your account and open the billing page. Choose Cancel plan. That’s it; no calls or emails needed.
  • You can also email support@squeezify.com and we’ll cancel it for you.
  • Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period you already paid for, then stops. You won’t be charged again.

Refunds

  • First payment: if Tube Search isn’t for you, email support@squeezify.com within 30 days of your first payment and we’ll refund that payment.
  • Renewals: monthly renewals aren’t refunded once the new month has started, but you can cancel any time to stop the next one.
  • Mistakes on our side: if you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or couldn’t use Tube Search because of a problem on our end, tell us and we’ll make it right.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and usually show up within 5 to 10 business days.
  • Where the law where you live gives you more rights (for example a cooling-off period), those rights apply.

Changing plans

You can move up or down between Basic, Pro and Premiere from your account. Upgrades start right away; Stripe adjusts the price for the rest of your billing period. Downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

Abuse

We may refuse refunds where an account has broken our Terms, for example by sharing the account or downloading large amounts of data to reuse.

Questions

Email support@squeezify.com. A real person answers.
